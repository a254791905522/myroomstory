🎮 About the Game

Step into cluttered 3D rooms and bring order to the chaos! In My Room Story, your mission is to drag and place scattered objects — furniture, decor, and themed items — into their correct shadow positions. Experience the satisfying snap as each piece locks perfectly into place. With strategic move limits, timer-based challenges, and helpful power-ups, every level is a fresh puzzle waiting to be solved.
